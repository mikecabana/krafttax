Questions

What is this?

A wait list for an app that would let anyone pay to have a box of Kraft Dinner delivered to the office of the mayor of Montreal, Soraya Martinez Ferrada. The app does not exist yet. This page measures whether enough people want it.

Do I pay anything now?

No. Joining the wait list is free and only takes an email address. No payment details are collected.

How much will a box cost?

The price is not set yet. It will cover the box, postage to Montreal city hall, and handling. People on the wait list will hear the price first.

What happens with my email?

It is used for one thing: to tell you if the app launches. It is not sold or shared.

Is this affiliated with Kraft Heinz or the City of Montreal?

No. This is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Kraft Heinz, the Ville de Montréal, or the mayor's office.
